Privacy
Policy

The fine print, minus the fine print.

Last updated: October 15, 2026

TL;DR We don’t collect your personal information on this site, we don’t track you, and we don’t sell anything to anyone. Here are the details.

Who we are

This website is operated by Coco Media Producciones (“Coco Media,” “we,” “us”), based in Miami, Florida and Chicago, Illinois. Questions about this policy go to our email.

What we collect

Nothing directly. This site has no forms, no accounts, no newsletter sign-up, and no analytics or advertising trackers. If you email us, we receive what you choose to send and use it only to reply to you.

Services this site relies on

A few third parties help the site work. They may receive standard technical information (like your IP address and browser type) when your browser loads their content:

  • Vimeo plays our videos. See Vimeo’s privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts serves our typefaces. See Google’s privacy policy.
  • Our web host may keep standard server logs for security and reliability.

Cookies and storage

We don’t set tracking cookies. The site saves one small note in your browser for the length of your visit so the loading animation doesn’t replay on every page. It contains no personal information and disappears when you close the tab.

Children

This site is meant for business visitors and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update it here and change the date at the top.
