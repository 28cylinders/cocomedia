Accessibility
Statement

Be Seen applies to everyone. Including here.

Last updated: October 15, 2026

TL;DR We make work so audiences can see themselves in it. That should start with being able to use our website.

Our standard

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA.

What we’ve done

  • Text and backgrounds meet AA color-contrast requirements.
  • Every page can be navigated with a keyboard, with visible focus and a “Skip to content” link.
  • Images have text alternatives, and decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
  • Moving content can be paused, and animations turn off if your device is set to reduce motion.
  • Pages work on phones, tablets, and desktop, and when zoomed to 200%.
  • Pages are tested with automated accessibility checks.

Known limits

Some videos are hosted on Vimeo, and captions depend on the original files. If you need a caption or transcript for something you see here, ask and we’ll help.

Tell us

If anything on this site is hard to use, please email our team. We’ll get back to you and work on a fix.
